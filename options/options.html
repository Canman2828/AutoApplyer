AI Model

Your API key is stored only in this browser (chrome.storage) and sent only to the provider you choose. For Anthropic the model defaults to claude-opus-4-8. "Custom" works with any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenRouter, Ollama, etc.).

Resume

📄

Your resume is stored only in this browser. It is automatically attached to Resume/CV upload fields when you fill an application, and it's sent along to your AI model so answers are grounded in it. Auto-fill profile reads the resume with your chosen AI model (API key required) and pre-fills the sections below — review the results, then click Save.

Workday Sign-in (optional)

Workday requires an account before you can apply. When "Fill this page" runs on a Workday sign-in or create-account page, these credentials are filled in directly. They are stored only in this browser, are never sent to the AI model, and are only ever filled on real Workday domains (myworkday.com / myworkdayjobs.com). Note: the password is stored unencrypted in extension storage — use a unique password here, not one you reuse elsewhere. New accounts may still require you to verify your email.
